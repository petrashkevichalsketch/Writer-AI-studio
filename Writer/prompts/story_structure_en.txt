━━━ SKELETON: TWO MODES ━━━

First choose total_chapters within book_spec.chapters_min..chapters_max.
The skeleton depends on the resulting number:

- total_chapters ≥ 9 → FULL skeleton: EXACTLY 15 Save the Cat beats (list below).
- total_chapters < 9 (a short story, a novella) → REDUCED skeleton:
  EXACTLY 5 beats — opening_image, catalyst, midpoint, all_is_lost, final_image.
  Do not include any other beats: the full skeleton does not fit into a short book.

━━━ MANDATORY BEATS (full skeleton) ━━━

The story_beats array contains EXACTLY 15 key points, distributed across
chapters from 1 to total_chapters.
Chapter numbers strictly increase, and each key appears exactly once:

1.  opening_image    — ch. ~1      — the hero's 'ordinary world' before everything changes
2.  theme_stated     — ch. ~2      — voicing the hidden theme / the story's lesson
3.  setup            — ch. ~2      — the environment, weaknesses, context
4.  catalyst         — ch. ~3      — the incident that breaks the status quo
5.  debate           — ch. ~4      — doubts and resistance to change
6.  break_into_two   — ch. ~5      — a deliberate step into the 'new world'
7.  b_story          — ch. ~6      — the secondary storyline (relationships/morale)
8.  fun_and_games    — ch. ~7      — delivering the genre promise (core premise)
9.  midpoint         — ch. ~9      — a false victory or a false defeat, the point of no return
10. bad_guys_close_in— ch. ~11     — mounting external and internal pressure
11. all_is_lost      — ch. ~14     — catastrophe, collapsed hopes, loss of footing
12. dark_night       — ch. ~15     — the existential pit, reflection
13. break_into_three — ch. ~15-16  — the insight: finding the solution through an inner shift
14. finale           — ch. ~16-17  — the climactic overcoming of obstacles
15. final_image      — ch. ~18     — the final mirror image (the changed world/hero)

Distribution rules:
- The first beat is opening_image (ch. 1), the last is final_image (ch. total_chapters).
- In both modes.
- Full skeleton: ≥ 2 chapters between break_into_two and midpoint; ≥ 3 chapters
  between midpoint and all_is_lost; ≥ 3 chapters between all_is_lost and
  final_image.
- Reduced skeleton: no gaps required, beats follow ascending chapter numbers.

━━━ COVERAGE — VERIFIED PROGRAMMATICALLY ━━━
