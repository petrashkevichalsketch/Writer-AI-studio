━━━ SKELETON: TWO MODES ━━━

First choose total_chapters within book_spec.chapters_min..chapters_max.
The skeleton depends on the resulting number:

- total_chapters ≥ 9 → FULL skeleton: EXACTLY 15 Save the Cat beats (list below).
- total_chapters < 9 (a short story, a novella) → REDUCED skeleton:
  EXACTLY 5 beats — opening_image, catalyst, midpoint, all_is_lost, final_image.
  Do not include any other beats: the full skeleton does not fit into a short book.

━━━ MANDATORY BEATS (full skeleton) ━━━

The story_beats array contains EXACTLY 15 key points, distributed across
chapters from 1 to total_chapters. Each key appears exactly once. The chapter
numbers below are given for 18 chapters; for a different total_chapters, scale
them proportionally while preserving the distribution rules.

1.  opening_image     — ch. ~1      — the initial 'minus' state: a deficiency, a false belief, or an absence. Not an 'ordinary world' but a world that already carries the seed of its future breakdown.
2.  theme_stated      — ch. ~2      — the theme is voiced not as a moral but as a question the hero currently answers wrongly.
3.  setup             — ch. ~2–3    — the hero's concrete limitations and weaknesses that will cost him dearly later.
4.  catalyst          — ch. ~3      — the incident that makes inaction costlier than action. The status quo is destroyed irreversibly.
5.  debate            — ch. ~4      — resistance to change. The hero chooses the old strategy and pays for it.
6.  break_into_two    — ch. ~5      — a deliberate step into the new mode. The old life is closed off. The last beat of Act I.
7.  b_story           — ch. ~6      — the second line (relationships / morale) as a mirror or counterweight to the main one, with a stake of its own. This stake must be resolved in break_into_three or finale.
8.  fun_and_games     — ch. ~7–8    — delivering the genre promise through action, not description. The hero tests the new strategy and gets first results (real or false).
9.  midpoint          — ch. ~9      — a false victory or a false defeat + a paradigm shift: the hero moves from reaction to initiative (or vice versa). The point of no return.
10. bad_guys_close_in — ch. ~11–12  — simultaneous growth of external pressure and internal crisis. The price of continuing rises.
11. all_is_lost       — ch. ~14     — catastrophe: loss of a key resource, ally, or conviction. Any hope of an 'easy' way out is destroyed.
12. dark_night        — ch. ~15     — the reaction to the catastrophe: the hero sees the price he has already paid and understands that the old self no longer works. A local dip in tension.
13. break_into_three  — ch. ~16     — the insight through an inner shift. The new solution is born from admitting the earlier mistake. The first beat of Act III.
14. finale            — ch. ~16–17  — the climactic overcoming. The hero acts from the new state; the external and internal lines converge.
15. final_image       — ch. ~18     — a mirror image: the new stable state, visually and functionally opposite to opening_image.

Distribution rules:
- The first beat is opening_image (ch. 1), the last is final_image (ch. total_chapters).
- Chapter numbers are non-strictly increasing: two neighboring beats in the list may share one chapter, three may not. Exception: dark_night and break_into_three are always in different chapters (dark_night earlier).
- Full skeleton: ≥ 2 chapters between break_into_two and midpoint; ≥ 3 chapters between midpoint and all_is_lost; ≥ 3 chapters between all_is_lost and final_image. When total_chapters < 14, each of these three gaps shrinks to 1 chapter.
- Reduced skeleton: no gaps required, beats follow ascending chapter numbers.

━━━ DESCRIPTION WORDING (ANTI-FILLER) ━━━

The length of a description is 2–3 sentences (up to ~60 words). For fun_and_games and bad_guys_close_in — up to ~80 words.

1. TEMPLATE. For opening_image, theme_stated, setup: 'State: …; deficiency: …'. For all other beats: 'Was → became (the fact that makes rollback impossible); price: …; inner: …'. The 'price' and 'inner' parts are mandatory only where items 2 and 3 require them.
2. PRICE. In the beats catalyst, break_into_two, midpoint, bad_guys_close_in, all_is_lost, finale (in the reduced skeleton — in those of them that are present), name a concrete loss or renunciation: a resource, a relationship, a conviction, a status. Refusing to pay is acceptable only if its consequence is named.
3. DOUBLE ARC. In the beats midpoint, all_is_lost, break_into_three, finale (in the reduced skeleton — midpoint and all_is_lost), the description reflects both the external goal and the internal line (false belief → crisis → new truth).
4. STEPS WITHIN A BEAT. In fun_and_games and bad_guys_close_in, list one step per chapter up to the next beat; each step is a separate shift, not a repetition of the previous one.
5. B-STORY PAYOFF. The description of break_into_three or finale must state how the b_story stake was resolved.
6. PROHIBITION. Do not phrase beats as 'show the world', 'reveal the character', 'build the atmosphere'. Only a vector of action and a change of state. Formula: 'X loses/gains Y, because of which Z'. Correct: 'The investigator is left without his only witness — the case collapses, and with it his confidence in his method'. Correct: 'The heiress signs away the house — there is no going back, and her family stops regarding her as one of their own'. Forbidden: 'The hero gets acquainted with the world'.
7. TURNING BEATS AND POV. Place the beats midpoint, all_is_lost, break_into_three, finale, where possible, on chapters of the main hero's thread (the main hero is the character of the first entry in character_arcs). If the chapter belongs to another thread, the description must show how the beat changes the main hero's state.

━━━ COVERAGE — VERIFIED PROGRAMMATICALLY ━━━

- acts: all chapters 1..total_chapters are distributed across acts, each EXACTLY once. Boundaries (full skeleton): Act I ends with the chapter of break_into_two inclusive; Act II runs up to the chapter preceding break_into_three; Act III begins with the chapter of break_into_three. Reduced skeleton: Act I — up to the chapter of catalyst inclusive, Act II — up to the chapter of all_is_lost inclusive, Act III — the rest.
- pov_threads: every chapter 1..total_chapters belongs to EXACTLY ONE thread — the chapters arrays of the threads do not overlap and together cover all chapters.
- reveal_schedule: EVERY secret from the input data must have an entry; foreshadow_from < reveal_at_chapter ≤ total_chapters.
- character_arcs: only existing characters. For the main hero, turns_at.chapter equals the chapter of the all_is_lost beat; for the others — within the range from midpoint to all_is_lost inclusive. ends_at.chapter equals the chapter of the final_image beat. starts_at — a concrete false belief or deficiency; turns_at.event — the event after which the old belief is no longer viable; ends_at.state — a new stable state, not 'the hero became better'.

━━━ SYNTAX REQUIREMENTS ━━━

1. JSON SYNTAX: Use the standard double quote `"` EXCLUSIVELY for JSON markup
(keys and string boundaries). Inside text values, use single quotes ' for
quotations and titles — a raw " inside a value breaks parsing. No trailing
commas (a comma after the last element of arrays/objects).
2. TENSION_CURVE: The array contains exactly total_chapters numbers from 0.0
to 1.0 (the schema example shows only the first three). Logic: a smooth start
(0.1–0.3); a rise toward the midpoint (~0.65–0.75); further growth up to
all_is_lost (a local peak of ~0.8–0.85); a dip at dark_night (~0.5); a final
peak at the chapter of finale (0.85–0.95), after which a decline is allowed.
In the reduced skeleton — the same logic, compressed to total_chapters points.
3. STRICT ID FORMAT: In the `character` field (in `pov_threads`) and the `char`
field (in `character_arcs`), you must use EXCLUSIVELY the technical character
IDs from the input data (e.g., `CH1`, `CH2`). It is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN
to use character names (e.g., `Silas Vane`) in these fields.
