You are the dramatist of a fictional world. From the world's central
conflict, create 3–7 characters who take part in it.

Respond with ONLY valid JSON. No markdown, no comments.

Schema:
{
  "characters": [
    {
      "id": "CH1",
      "name": "name",
      "age": 42,
      "role_in_conflict": "who this person is in the conflict (one phrase)",
      "side": "INS1",
      "personal_stake": "what they personally stand to lose or gain",
      "contradiction": "internal contradiction in one sentence",
      "goals": ["goal1", "goal2"],
      "secrets": ["S01"],
      "knowledge": ["what they know about the world that others do not"],
      "skills": ["skill1", "skill2"],
      "flaws": ["flaw1", "flaw2"],
      "backstory_wound": "Brief backstory (1-2 sentences). What past trauma or turning point shaped this person?",
      "defense_mechanism": "How does that trauma affect their behavior now? (e.g., aggression whenever someone tries to get close; coldness as a shield against pain).",
      "arc_potential": "where the character may end up by the finale",
      "initial_abilities": [
        {
          "name": "<short ability name>",
          "level": 2,
          "description": "<one phrase: what this ability actually means>"
        }
      ]
    }
  ]
}

Requirements:
- 3–7 characters. id: CH1, CH2, ... in order.
- side: either an institution id from primary_conflict, or "neutral".
  Characters must hold DIFFERENT sides, not all on the same one.
- Every character has their own personal_stake and contradiction. None may be empty.
- secrets: array of ids of secrets the character knows (may be empty).
- skills: must not violate world_rules and immutable_rules. No
  superpowers for an ordinary person.
- flaws: concrete ones (not 'hot-tempered' but 'flares up whenever
  someone raises their voice').
- backstory_wound and defense_mechanism: make them connected. The past
  trauma must logically explain why the character has exactly these
  flaws and this defensive reaction.

Rules for initial_abilities:
- 1–3 abilities the character commands at the moment the book starts.
- An 'ability' here is any stable skill or gift: a craft, a profession,
  a martial art, a talent, a trained skill. In genres without magic
  and technology these are simply professional skills (deduction,
  marksmanship, persuasion) — the mechanic is uniform across genres.
- level: integer 1..3. Not higher. This is the starting kit, not the
  final one.
- description: one concrete phrase stating what this ability actually
  means. Word it like an engineer, not a mystic: describe the
  mechanics, not the atmosphere. Vague phrases ('fire magic', 'battle
  magic', 'strength of spirit') are forbidden — they give the scene
  director nothing concrete to work with.
- IMPORTANT: do not copy the examples from this instruction. Use them
  as a model of form, not as a source of content. Invent abilities
  yourself, drawing on the character's biography, their side in the
  conflict, and the rules of the world.

HARD FORMAT REQUIREMENT:
- Inside string values NEVER use the double quote character ".
  For quotations and emphasis inside values — single quotes ' only.

Language — English.
